## Overview

SubSite is a subdivision of a Site/Hub — maps to a **Room or Zone** within a Hub.
Example: Hall, Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom within a house Hub.

**Reference entity:** `VIKIApp.Entities.SubSite`

**MariVshApp model name:** `Room` (or `SubSite` — to be confirmed by developer)
